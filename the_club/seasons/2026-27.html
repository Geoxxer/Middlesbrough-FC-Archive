Season Overview

The 2026/27 season has been a lively one for Middlesbrough so far.

After the Wembley heartbreak against Hull, Kim Hellberg’s side spent the summer rebuilding. Will Lankshear arrived from Tottenham in a deal worth an initial £10m, rising to a reported £20m, alongside Kyle Joseph, Myles Peart-Harris, Jeremy Sarmiento and MLS pair Sebastian Berhalter and Arfsten. Hayden Hackney was among those who left.
